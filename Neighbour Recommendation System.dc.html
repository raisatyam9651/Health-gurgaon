Neighbour Recommendations: ranking doctors by what residents say

Doctors in each locality are ordered by how often different residents recommend them positively in forum discussions, weighted for trust and recency and balanced against negative experiences.

KEY DECISIONDon't rank by raw mention count. It rewards whoever posts most, including staff, relatives and paid accounts. Count unique, trusted residents instead. The demo below shows the difference.
