What stays national
Name structure, circle of neighbours, marigold, type, layout, components, verification badges. Moving from Health Gurgaon to Health Delhi feels like the same trusted product.
What becomes local
City colour, the local heart, hero photography, locality names, events and copy. Each heart also becomes a pattern for posters, banners and WhatsApp graphics.
Before finalising
Have residents of each city review their local heart; references such as the Charminar and kolam should be checked for respectful use. Test city colours for contrast as text on cream (all are currently above 4.5:1 by design).
