1Brand directions: three distinct identities for the Health[City] network
RECOMMENDED ARCHITECTURE · HYBRID
One platform, one master domain, city editions branded “Health Gurgaon”, “Health Delhi”…
City content lives at /gurgaon, /delhi on one domain so authority compounds. HealthGurgaon.in etc. 301-redirect to their edition and act as memorable shortcuts on posters and WhatsApp. Same logo structure everywhere — only the city name and accent change.
NAMING · TO VALIDATE
City-facing name: Health[City]. Parent placeholder: “Health Cities Network”.
Challenge: the city is officially Gurugram (since 2016) but most residents still say Gurgaon. Keep “Health Gurgaon” as the brand, use “Gurugram” in page copy and metadata, and check search volume for both before launch. Domain and trademark availability still to confirm.
GROUND RULES FOR ALL THREE
— No medical cross, heart or location pin used as-is.
— Symbol must survive 16px favicon and a WhatsApp circle crop.
— City accent swaps; ink, paper and status colours stay fixed.
— Devanagari support built in from day one.
