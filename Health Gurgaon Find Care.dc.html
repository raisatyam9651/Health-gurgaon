Paediatricians in Gurgaon

Doctors who specialise in the health of babies, children and teenagers: check-ups, vaccinations, common illnesses and growth concerns.

EXPLAINER REVIEWED BY DR. S. MALHOTRA, MD PAEDIATRICS · UPDATED 2 OCT 2026
When to see a paediatricianRoutine check-ups and vaccinations, fever lasting more than two days, feeding or growth worries, rashes, ear pain, persistent cough.
Go to emergency, don't wait for an appointment, if your childhas trouble breathing, a seizure, is unusually drowsy or hard to wake, or a baby under 3 months has a fever. Call 112.
