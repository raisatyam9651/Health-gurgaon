Fixed across all cities · Lexend + Mukta + Spline Sans Mono
Typography

Lexend was designed specifically to reduce visual stress and improve reading speed. Its wide letter spacing and open shapes suit senior citizens, caregivers reading on the go and anyone checking a clinic address on a small phone. One family covers everything from the wordmark to the fine print.

Mukta pairs with it for Hindi and Marathi: similar stroke weight and open counters, so bilingual lines sit together without one script shouting.

Spline Sans Mono is used only for dates, times, fees and verification stamps, so the facts look like facts.

What stays national
Name structure, circle of neighbours, marigold, type, layout, components, verification badges. Moving from Health Gurgaon to Health Delhi feels like the same trusted product.
What becomes local
City colour, the local heart, hero photography, locality names, events and copy. Each heart also becomes a pattern for posters, banners and WhatsApp graphics.
Before finalising
Have residents of each city review their local heart; references such as the Charminar and kolam should be checked for respectful use. Test city colours for contrast as text on cream (all are currently above 4.5:1 by design).
